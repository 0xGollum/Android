## <img src="assets/xlight-icon.jpg" width="34"> X Light — Flashlight & Torch

A bright LED flashlight that turns on in **one tap** — and looks like *you* want it to.
**500+ button themes** with matching glow colors, a **home-screen widget**, a torch that **stays on in the background**, a lighter-style click with a short vibration, and **11 languages**. Free, no account, no sign-up. The only permission is the camera, to control the flash.

## <img src="assets/ike-icon.png" width="34"> Ike — Tasks & Priorities

The **Eisenhower matrix** in your pocket: every task lands in one of four boxes (urgent / important), with a to-do list inside each task. Tick, archive, restore from the bin, share, and **back up your data**. **11 languages**, made to keep things clear when your head is busy.

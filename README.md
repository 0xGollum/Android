## 🔦 X Light — Flashlight & Torch

<img src="assets/xlight-banner.png" width="100%">

A bright LED flashlight that turns on in **one tap** — and looks like *you* want it to.
**500+ button themes** with matching glow colors, a **home-screen widget**, a torch that
**stays on in the background**, a lighter-style click with a short vibration, and **11 languages**.
Free, no account, no sign-up. The only permission is the camera — to control the flash.
